# Phase 4.5 — Tracks

<Callout type="note" title="Planning baseline">
  Tracks group milestones for planning. Preferred sequencing below is not a rigid schedule.
  Paths, libraries, and designs on linked milestone pages are orientation sketches — implementation
  is expected to require further research and may diverge.
</Callout>

Phase 4.5 delivers the intelligence layer — behavioral fingerprinting, drift detection, and directive regression testing — across four tracks.

---

## Track A — Behavioral Fingerprinting

Feature extraction pipeline, fingerprint schema, and trigger/scheduling for computing behavioral baselines per agent. Statistical feature choices are a starting preference and may be revised after calibration research.

**Preferred sequencing within track:** `4.5.A.1 → 4.5.A.2 → 4.5.A.3`

**Milestones:**

- [4.5.A.2 — Fingerprint Schema & Migration](/roadmap/phase-4-5-intelligence-layer/milestones/4.5.a.2-fingerprint-schema-migration)
- [4.5.A.3 — Fingerprint Trigger & Scheduling](/roadmap/phase-4-5-intelligence-layer/milestones/4.5.a.3-fingerprint-trigger-scheduling)
