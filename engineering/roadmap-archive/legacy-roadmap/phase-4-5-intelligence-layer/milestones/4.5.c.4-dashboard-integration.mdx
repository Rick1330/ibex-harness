## Why This Milestone Exists

Completes the directive management UX from the spec — version timeline, diff viewer, regression scenarios with pass/fail/critical-fail states, and a live rollout progress view that didn't exist in the original UX spec because gradual rollout was never designed with a mechanism until Milestone 4.5.C.3.
