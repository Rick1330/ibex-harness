## Why This Milestone Exists

The highest-value Playwright scenario in the redesigned roadmap — the first place three previously-separate subsystems (fingerprinting, drift, directive regression) are proven to work as one operator-facing workflow through the dashboard.

---

## E2E Scenarios

**API-level (`make e2e-smoke-p4.5`):** Seed agent → 30 synthetic sessions with mid-stream behavior shift → verify fingerprint → drift alert → regression run → promotion blocked pending review.

**Playwright:** Operator opens drift alert → clicks "run regression from here" → watches live pass/fail stream → approves → promotes with gradual rollout → observes rollout progress advancing → manually pauses → resumes.
