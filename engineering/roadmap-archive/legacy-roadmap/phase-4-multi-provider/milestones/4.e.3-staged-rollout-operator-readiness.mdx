# Milestone 4.E.3 — Staged Rollout & Operator Readiness

**Status:** Planned — promotion consumes E1/E2 and P6 evidence.
**Outcome:** The platform is promoted safely with observation, stop conditions, on-call ownership, and supportability.
**Track:** [Track E — Release Readiness & Phase Exit](/roadmap/phase-4-multi-provider/tracks#track-e--release-readiness--phase-exit)
**Dependencies:** 4.E.1, 4.E.2, 4.P.5, 4.P.6, Track D scope
**Estimated effort:** 2 weeks
**Gate:** G3 promotion

<Callout type="note" title="Promotion evidence">
  A stage may advance only when the P6 artifact manifest, required-check inventory, E1 operator-E2E bundle, E2 resilience bundle, and rollback record identify the same commit, digests, schema, environment, and fixture versions. Static documentation or compatibility-shell smoke may demonstrate availability only; it cannot authorize promotion.
</Callout>

## Scope and deliverables

Execute dark launch (internal) → 1% → 10% → 25% → 100% with bake windows and thresholds for error rate, p99, SSE lag, queue depth, cost anomaly, auth failures, deletion backlog, cache isolation, and tenant-negative results. Publish on-call runbook, escalation tree, kill switches, support bundle, and an immutable promotion ledger entry per stage.

Each entry links P6/E1/E2 artifacts, image/artifact digest, schema/configuration version, owner, metric snapshot, go/no-go rationale, and tested rollback command. High-impact actions remain disabled unless their P1/P3 evidence is present.

## Verification and rollback

Abort and rollback must be exercised during the promotion exercise. The post-promotion window must have no unowned P0/P1 breach. A guardrail breach halts the stage and restores the prior immutable digest; the operator origin can be disabled without taking down public docs.

## Findings addressed

F4-010, F4-011, F4-014.

## Exit checklist

- [ ] Stage ladder and bake windows are executed or signed off as an explicit exception.
- [ ] P6, E1, and E2 manifests link to each promotion stage.
- [ ] Abort/rollback transcript and kill-switch inventory are attached.
- [ ] On-call support bundle is reviewed.
- [ ] Post-promotion observation completes without an unowned P0/P1 breach.
