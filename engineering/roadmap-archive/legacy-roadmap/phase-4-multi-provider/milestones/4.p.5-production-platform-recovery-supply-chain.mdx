# Milestone 4.P.5 — Production Platform, Recovery & Supply Chain

**Status:** In progress / partial — repository foundations do not equal production closure.
**Outcome:** The operator system is deployable, recoverable, observable, and promotable only after environment-specific evidence exists.
**Track:** [Track P — Operator Readiness, Evidence & Control Plane](/roadmap/phase-4-multi-provider/tracks#track-p--operator-readiness-evidence--control-plane)
**Dependencies:** 4.P.0; 4.P.2
**Estimated effort:** 3 weeks
**Gate:** G5 platform (partial)

<Callout type="warning" title="Open production evidence">
  Do not claim P5 complete from chart files, compose scripts, or CI policy text. The inspected readiness baseline does not verify an `console` workload, production Ingress/gateway, TLS/cookie contract, clean-cluster promotion, real pgBackRest PITR, measured RPO/RTO, or a live Kyverno admission soak. These remain required evidence, not implied implementation.
</Callout>

## Scope and present boundary

The intended platform contract covers digest-pinned workloads, probes, PDB/HPA, migration compatibility, rollback by digest, encrypted backups, restore and evidence-bundle recovery, backup freshness, signed images, Kyverno `verifyImages`, worker time limits, and platform health. Helm is the intended deployment mechanism; no Kustomize decision is required.

The console workload, service ownership, Ingress/gateway routing, TLS termination and renewal, browser promotion path, and production values must be specified and evidenced before the canonical operator runtime can be promoted. `services/dashboard/` remains compatibility-only and is not a substitute workload.

## Recovery and supply-chain contract

Record target RPO/RTO per store only after a measured drill. The working planning targets are Postgres ≤5m/≤30m, ClickHouse ≤24h/≤60m, Redis as cache/no recovery target, MinIO ≤24h/≤60m, and outbox RPO 0 by sequence; these are targets, not evidence. Restore reports must include tenant-isolation queries, data classes, achieved RPO/RTO, image/schema/config digests, and rollback command. Admission reports must show unsigned-image rejection in a real staging cluster.

## Verification gate and residual register

Keep Helm/chart and any compose restore evidence linked, but leave explicit open rows for console workload, Ingress, TLS, real PITR, measured RPO/RTO, and live Kyverno admission. Load/chaos verification belongs to 4.E.2. P6 consumes only artifact-backed runtime evidence.

## Rollout and rollback

**Trigger:** failed restore, missing ingress/TLS, probe failure, digest mismatch, admission failure, or RPO/RTO breach. **Reversal:** roll back to a known-good digest and compatible schema/configuration, or disable the operator origin; never reverse an incompatible migration by data surgery. **Verify:** restore, tenant-isolation, admission, and runtime smoke artifacts are attached.

## Findings addressed

F4-011, F4-020, F4-032.

## Exit checklist

- [ ] Helm deployment evidence includes the canonical console workload.
- [ ] Ingress/gateway, TLS, cookie, and SSE timeout contracts are proven in staging.
- [ ] Real pgBackRest PITR restore and measured RPO/RTO report are attached.
- [ ] Live Kyverno unsigned-image rejection is evidenced; CI policy text alone is insufficient.
- [ ] Digest rollback and dependency-aware health/drain are exercised.
- [ ] Open rows are consumed by 4.E.2/4.E.3 rather than marked closed by prose.
