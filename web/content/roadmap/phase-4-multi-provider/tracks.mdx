# Phase 4 — Tracks

<Callout type="note" title="Sequencing contract">
  Phase 4 uses typed gates. Preferred sequencing is a dependency model, not a promise that every milestone is serial. Capability and assurance work may proceed in parallel when their inputs and evidence outputs are explicit.
</Callout>
