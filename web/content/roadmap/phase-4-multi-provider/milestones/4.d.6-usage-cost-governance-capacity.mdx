# Milestone 4.D.6 — Usage, Cost Governance & Capacity

**Status:** Planned  
**Outcome:** Operators explain spend and enforce budgets without double charging or relying on ephemeral counters.  
**Track:** [Track D — Operator Capability Slices](/roadmap/phase-4-multi-provider/tracks#track-d--operator-capability-slices)  
**Dependencies:** 4.D.1; 4.D.2 recommended for pivots; 4.P.2 durable facts; 4.P.4 ledger/query; 4.P.5 resource controls  
**Estimated effort:** 3 weeks

<Callout type="note" title="Completion contract">
  Analytics becomes governance only when persisted facts can change authorization decisions and prove the result (F4-005).
</Callout>

## Why this milestone exists

Charts over ephemeral counters are not hard caps.

## Scope and deliverables

Usage ledger UI, versioned rate cards, provider/model/fallback attribution, rollups, budget scopes/periods, alert state, hard pause/degrade decisions, Redis hot counters with reconciliation, worker concurrency and queue dashboards, resource/cost ceilings, exportable cost evidence.

## Contract and data requirements

Idempotent ingestion; raw-to-aggregate reconciliation; retry does not double charge; policy-version replay; hard cap blocks proxy requests; completeness labels on live vs historical spend. All queries scoped by `org_id`.

## Product and UX requirements

Visible rate-card/policy versions; soft vs hard enforcement state; alert-only vs deny.

## Non-functional requirements

High-cardinality query remains bounded; concurrent over-admission tests.

## Verification gates

Hard-cap negative path on proxy; reconciliation report; rate-card replay; no double charge on retry.

## Rollout and rollback

**Trigger:** cost/governance false denies, chart-to-ledger mismatch, or tenant-limit regressions. **Reversal:** return affected tenants to soft or alert-only limits; stop hard governance denies from the new path. **Verify:** reconcile enforcement decisions; charts remain informational; soft/alert-only limits still fire before re-enabling hard governance.

## Findings addressed

F4-005.
