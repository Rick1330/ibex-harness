# Milestone 4.D.6 — Usage, Cost Governance & Capacity

**Status:** Planned  
**Outcome:** Operators explain spend and enforce budgets without double charging or relying on ephemeral counters.  
**Track:** [Track D — Operator Capability Slices](/roadmap/phase-4-multi-provider/tracks#track-d--operator-capability-slices)  
**Dependencies:** Track P gates and preceding Track D slice  
**Estimated effort:** 3 weeks

<Callout type="note" title="Completion contract">
  This milestone is complete only when the implementation, versioned contract, verification evidence, operational signals, and rollback behavior described below are present. A mock screen, planning-only schema, permission constant, or passing unit test alone is not sufficient evidence.
</Callout>

## Why this milestone exists

Operators explain spend and enforce budgets without double charging or relying on ephemeral counters.

## Scope and deliverables

Build usage/cost tables, rate-card provenance, provider/model/fallback attribution, rollups, budget scopes, alerts, soft/hard caps, queue/resource dashboards, capacity limits, reconciliation, and evidence export.

## Contract and data requirements

Usage ledger, rate-card, budget decision, proxy authorization, Redis reconciliation, and resource policy contracts are required.

## Product and UX requirements

Show facts versus estimates, freshness, sampling, policy version, allocation, and enforcement state.

## Non-functional requirements

Raw-to-aggregate reconciliation, retry dedupe, hard-cap denial, high-cardinality query, and load/cost ceilings pass.

## Verification gates

Shadow and alert-only first; soft limits then hard enforcement per tenant.

## Rollout and rollback

Charts become governance only when durable facts can change authorization decisions.

## Findings addressed

This milestone addresses the repository and roadmap findings recorded in Track P and the Phase 4 findings register.
