# Milestone 4.P.3 — Privacy, Retention, Deletion & Audit Governance

**Status:** Planned

## Why this milestone exists

Sensitive evidence is useful without weakening existing content-omission and tenant boundaries.

## Scope and deliverables

Define none/metadata/redacted/full capture policy. Redact before queue/export. Store privileged payloads in encrypted object storage with manifests. Add retention policy, purge, deletion by trace/session/org, legal hold, tombstones, per-store watermarks, receipts, audit ledger, safe rendering, and signed evidence bundles.

## Contract and data requirements

Capture manifests include policy version, redaction state, retention class, digest, legal hold, deletion state, and chain of custody. Audit records include actor, session, purpose, object, fields, approval, result, and correlation IDs.

## Product and UX requirements

Raw data is hidden by default. Prompts, outputs, tool arguments, links, HTML, Markdown, and model-suggested commands render as inert escaped data.

## Non-functional requirements

Secrets never reach analytical stores, logs, queues, dead letters, or browser action handlers. Deletion normal query paths converge to no data and retries are safe.

## Verification gates

Run redaction, prompt-injection, cross-tenant, export, deletion, legal-hold, audit-tamper, and evidence-bundle verification tests.

## Rollout and rollback

Begin metadata-only. Enable redacted fields per tenant. Raw capture is time-limited and independently killable. Deletion is not rolled back.
