### Non-erasable allowlist (for 4.P.4)

Worker constant `NON_ERASABLE_STORES` is empty today. Future usage/billing ledgers must be named here; the saga refuses those store names. Documented for Track D / 4.P.4 consumers.

### ClickHouse TTL residual

MergeTree TTL cannot be paused per-row without table redesign. Mutations honor legal hold (hold gate before any `ALTER DELETE`). TTL may still age rows while a hold is active — honest residual until a hold-aware TTL strategy exists.

### Model-policy epoch spike

After PG policy delete, the saga PUBLISHes `model_policy_updates:{org_id}` with a bumped epoch so proxy cache cannot reinstall. Residual gap (if any) is invalidate-only — no rewrite of 4.P.1 packages beyond the worker invalidate call.
