# Milestone 4.P.3 — Privacy, Retention, Deletion & Audit Governance

**Status:** Planned  
**Outcome:** Evidence is useful without reversing the repository’s no-prompt/no-completion security boundary; deletion and retention are coordinated, hold-aware, and receipted.  
**Track:** [Track P — Operator Readiness, Evidence & Control Plane](/roadmap/phase-4-multi-provider/tracks#track-p--operator-readiness-evidence--control-plane)  
**Dependencies:** 4.P.1; 4.P.2  
**Estimated effort:** 3 weeks  
**Gate:** G4 governance

<Callout type="note" title="Completion contract">
  ClickHouse TTL is storage hygiene, not a deletion SLA (F4-004). Org delete that stops at Postgres is a P0 defect (F4-019).
</Callout>

## Why this milestone exists

GDPR erasure, incident reconstruction, and safe debugging require a coordinated data-lifecycle contract.

## Scope and deliverables

- Capture modes: none, metadata-only, redacted, full — per tenant, field, environment, and event kind. Redact before queueing or exporting.
- Encrypted object storage for privileged payloads with manifest, retention class, masking policy version, digest.
- Tenant retention policies, purge schedules, delete-by-trace/session/org, export-before-delete, legal hold, tombstones, per-store watermarks, deletion receipts.
- Cross-store org deletion (Postgres, ClickHouse, Redis, MinIO/object, queues, derived projections) that does not report success until receipted.
- Append-only audit ledger (actor, session, purpose, policy result, object, fields, approval, before/after hashes, correlation IDs).
- Safe rendering: prompts/outputs/tool args/links/HTML/Markdown are inert escaped data; strict CSP.
- `evidence_bundle` manifest with digests, versions, redacted refs, chain-of-custody, retention tier, signature.

## Contract and data requirements

Field/action policy for raw-read, export, delete (F4-006). Deletion jobs carry soft/hard time limits (F4-020). Every store applies `org_id` authorization.

## Product and UX requirements

Operators see retention horizon, hold state, deletion job status, and receipt IDs. Missing/redacted/expired evidence is labeled — never inferred as empty success.

## Non-functional requirements

Masked-by-default fixtures prove secrets never reach ClickHouse, OTLP, logs, DLQs, or browser actions. Deletion retries are safe and idempotent.

## Verification gates

Injection fixtures with hidden instructions/HTML/links. Deletion tests prove normal query paths return no data after completion across all stores. Audit writes tamper-evident and read-restricted.

## Rollout and rollback

**Trigger:** deletion incompleteness, secret leak, or unsafe render. **Reversal:** begin metadata-only; kill raw capture independently; deletion is never rolled back — restore requires governed import. **Verify:** cross-store deletion receipt for a test org; hostile-content render suite green.
