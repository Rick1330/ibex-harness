## Why this milestone exists

An operator can answer why an agent acted without first selecting an agent or session.

## Scope and deliverables

Build global query bar, facets, trace/session/failure results, progressive disclosure, timeline/flame graph, context assembly, memory score vectors, directive/routing decisions, tools, model/fallback, evaluation links, redacted JSON, copy-link, and bidirectional pivots.

## Contract and data requirements

Trace/Span DTOs expose stable IDs, source links, completeness, sampling, freshness, retention, and org-enforced scope.

## Product and UX requirements

Summary strip, candidate matrix, score/rank/resource/outcome lanes, exact selected-item waterfall/tree, and exclusion groups follow the design guide.

## Non-functional requirements

Golden nested run, source-link coverage, cross-tenant negative, metadata-only default, query/page-load budget, and keyboard-safe detail pass.

## Verification gates

Metadata-only launch, then redacted provenance. Feature-flag event kinds and content expansion.

## Rollout and rollback

This is the differentiated auditable context-and-policy provenance debugger.

## Findings addressed

This milestone addresses the repository and roadmap findings recorded in Track P and the Phase 4 findings register.
