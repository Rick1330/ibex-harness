## Why This Milestone Exists

A permanent regression gate proves the Lua hierarchical limiter is atomic under concurrency — the property the Phase 1 soft slider could not guarantee. The proof is binary: 200 concurrent `Check`s against RPM 100 must admit **exactly** 100 and reject **exactly** 100 (`maxAdmitOvershoot=0`) at every binding tier.

- Per-tier exact admit (agent / org / global): binding tier RPM=100, others high; 200 concurrent `Check`s; exactly 100 allowed / 100 denied; `DeniedTier` matches.
