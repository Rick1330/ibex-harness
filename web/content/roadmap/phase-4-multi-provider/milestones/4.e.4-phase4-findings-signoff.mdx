## Why this milestone exists

Phase 4 closes with signed evidence, zero open P0 gaps, and owned residual risk.

## Scope and deliverables

Publish findings register with severity, evidence, owner, due date, exception rationale, and links to CI/recovery/security artifacts. Record architecture decisions, runbooks, support docs, and release certificate.

## Contract and data requirements

The sign-off schema references each Track P/D/E milestone, commit, artifact, environment, and residual risk.

## Product and UX requirements

Provide an operator-facing status page for known limitations and evidence freshness.

## Non-functional requirements

No open P0. Every P1/P2 has owner/date or explicit accepted exception.

## Verification gates

Sign-off is immutable. Corrections create a new finding or amendment.

## Rollout and rollback

This replaces a generic exit audit with an evidence-backed governance record.

## Findings addressed

This milestone addresses the repository and roadmap findings recorded in Track P and the Phase 4 findings register.

- [ ] The stated outcome is demonstrated against real or explicitly labeled deterministic fixtures.
- [ ] The API, event, schema, or configuration contract is versioned and covered by compatibility tests.
- [ ] Tenant, permission, privacy, freshness, and partial-data behavior are verified.
- [ ] Performance, accessibility, failure, and rollback evidence is attached to the milestone record.
- [ ] Documentation, runbooks, and links are updated before the milestone is marked complete.
