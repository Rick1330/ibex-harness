# Milestone 4.D.4 — Failures, Incidents & Evidence Bundles

**Status:** Planned  
**Outcome:** An operator triages failures, assigns ownership, preserves evidence, and exports a postmortem-ready bundle.  
**Track:** [Track D — Operator Capability Slices](/roadmap/phase-4-multi-provider/tracks#track-d--operator-capability-slices)  
**Dependencies:** Track P gates and preceding Track D slice  
**Estimated effort:** 3 weeks

  This milestone is complete only when the implementation, versioned contract, verification evidence, operational signals, and rollback behavior described below are present. A mock screen, planning-only schema, permission constant, or passing unit test alone is not sufficient evidence.
</Callout>

## Why this milestone exists

An operator triages failures, assigns ownership, preserves evidence, and exports a postmortem-ready bundle.

## Scope and deliverables

Build failure projection, incident lifecycle, severity, owner, assignment, dedupe, timeline, activity, linked traces/sessions/requests, redacted evidence, comments, and signed bundle export.

## Contract and data requirements

State transitions, action idempotency, evidence manifest, retention tier, legal hold, and chain of custody are API contracts.

## Product and UX requirements

Filters by status/severity/owner/time, explicit raw traceback gating, and clear incomplete-evidence states.

## Non-functional requirements

Transitions are audited and idempotent. Bundles remain reconstructable after normal telemetry expiry without secrets or cross-tenant data.

## Verification gates

Read-only triage first. Mutations use operator action ledger and reversible state changes.

## Rollout and rollback

The existing failed-task store is ingestion/forensics data, not an incident workflow. **Trigger:** incident write-path regressions or evidence-bundle integrity failures. **Reversal:** return incident workflows to read-only triage and stop mutating incident state. **Verify:** existing bundles and recovery evidence remain readable; no new write actions succeed until rollback is cleared.

## Findings addressed

This milestone addresses the repository and roadmap findings recorded in Track P and the Phase 4 findings register.
