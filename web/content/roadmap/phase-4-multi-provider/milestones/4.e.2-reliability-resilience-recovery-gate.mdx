# Milestone 4.E.2 — Reliability, Resilience & Recovery Gate

**Status:** planned/specification — consumes resilience evidence; no resilience result is claimed here.
**Outcome:** Declared SLO, RPO, RTO, cost, resilience, and rollback guarantees are measured in staging.
**Track:** [Track E — Release Readiness & Phase Exit](/roadmap/phase-4-multi-provider/tracks#track-e--release-readiness--phase-exit)
**Dependencies:** resilience evidence from 4.C.5 and P5, plus the relevant P6-slice-certification outputs. E2 does not depend on E1; E3 follows E1 and E2.
**Estimated effort:** 3 weeks
**Gate:** G2 resilience

<Callout type="note" title="Evidence contract">
  E2 consumes P6 performance, SSE, cache-isolation, tenant-negative, artifact-manifest, and rollback outputs. A docs/static-shell smoke cannot satisfy this gate. P5's absent PITR, RPO/RTO, Kyverno, Ingress, or TLS evidence remains open until a staging experiment records it.
</Callout>

## Scope and experiments

Run sustained operator query/SSE load with p50/p95/p99, error rate, throughput, cost, memory, queue lag, and SSE lag thresholds. Exercise one-byte-per-second consumers, bounded buffers, write deadlines, shedding, reconnect/resume, duplicate suppression, and drain. Inject provider mid-stream failure, Redis/ClickHouse/Postgres degradation, queue saturation, slow consumers, pod kill, and network partition. Execute backup/restore and rollback with tenant isolation, evidence/outbox position, achieved RPO/RTO, and immutable digest/configuration records.

Each experiment records workload, threshold, observation window, error budget, tenant invariants, degraded mode, stop condition, owner, and P6-slice-certification artifact links. Missing data is labeled incomplete, never zero.

## Verification and rollback

The gate requires applicable P6-slice-certification artifacts to be complete and parser-valid, no cache cross-talk, no SSE memory growth under the declared soak, post-restore tenant isolation, and rollback service within declared RTO. Failed experiments create owned findings and halt promotion.

**Trigger:** SLO breach, restore failure, or rollback failure. **Reversal:** halt promotion, disable the operator origin or route to the last known-good digest, and preserve the evidence bundle. **Verify:** rerun the affected P6 check and append the finding disposition.

## Exit checklist

- [ ] Applicable P6-slice-certification performance and slow-client reports meet declared thresholds.
- [ ] SSE reconnect/resume, shedding, and drain evidence is attached.
- [ ] Provider/dependency degradation and queue saturation are recorded.
- [ ] Restore report includes achieved RPO/RTO and tenant-negative checks.
- [ ] Chaos/rollback transcript uses an immutable digest and links the P6-slice-certification manifest.
- [ ] P5 open platform evidence is implemented-with-evidence or accepted as residual-risk.
