# Milestone 4.P.5 — Production Platform, Recovery & Supply Chain

**Status:** Planned  
**Outcome:** The operator system is deployable, recoverable, observable, and promotable with measured RPO/RTO and supply-chain verification.  
**Track:** [Track P — Operator Readiness, Evidence & Control Plane](/roadmap/phase-4-multi-provider/tracks#track-p--operator-readiness-evidence--control-plane)  
**Dependencies:** 4.P.0; 4.P.2 (outbox/data plane awareness)  
**Estimated effort:** 3 weeks  
**Gate:** G5 platform

<Callout type="note" title="Completion contract">
  Architecture prose is not a deployment. Executable overlays and a clean-environment restore drill are required (F4-011, F4-032).
</Callout>

## Why this milestone exists

Without deploy/recover/admit evidence, Track D cannot be production-ready.

## Scope and deliverables

- Helm/Kustomize (or equivalent) overlays for UI, API, workers, relays, ClickHouse, Redis, dependencies.
- Digest-pinned images; startup/liveness/readiness probes; drain; requests/limits; autoscaling bounds; migration ordering; canary/blue-green; automatic abort; rollback.
- Encrypted off-cluster Postgres backups + WAL/PITR; restore scripts; backup freshness alerts; evidence-bundle recovery; measured RPO/RTO covering Postgres, ClickHouse, Redis, object manifests, outbox.
- SBOM, provenance, signatures, admission verification; deployed digest recorded in evidence bundles.
- Worker soft/hard time limits for deletion/maintenance tasks (F4-020).

## Contract and data requirements

Operators can query dependency health, last backup, last restore drill, retention horizon, ingestion lag, DLQ depth, degraded-mode state.

## Product and UX requirements

Overview surfaces platform health/freshness inputs consumed by 4.D.1.

## Non-functional requirements

Load and chaos: peak+headroom, Redis/ClickHouse/Postgres degradation, provider failure, slow consumers, queue saturation, rollback. SLOs: latency percentiles, availability, error rate, throughput, queue lag, cost budgets.

## Verification gates

Clean-environment restore meets RPO/RTO and preserves tenant isolation. Admission rejects unsigned/wrong-digest images in staging.

## Rollout and rollback

**Trigger:** failed restore, probe failure, or admission mismatch. **Reversal:** roll back by digest and known-good migration-compatible version; never roll back a migration without its compatibility procedure. **Verify:** restore drill report + canary abort transcript attached.

## Findings addressed

F4-011, F4-020, F4-032.

## Exit checklist

- [ ] Overlays committed and documented.
- [ ] Restore drill report with RPO/RTO attached.
- [ ] Supply-chain/admission evidence attached.
- [ ] Probe/drain/rollback runbook linked.
