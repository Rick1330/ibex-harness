# Milestone 4.P.5 — Production Platform, Recovery & Supply Chain

**Status:** Planned  
**Outcome:** The operator system is deployable, recoverable, observable, and promotable.  
**Track:** [Track P — Operator Readiness, Evidence & Control Plane](/roadmap/phase-4-multi-provider/tracks#track-p--operator-readiness-evidence--control-plane)  
**Dependencies:** Track A identity/API foundations; Phase 3.5; 4.C.5 baseline  
**Estimated effort:** 4 weeks

<Callout type="note" title="Completion contract">
  This milestone is complete only when the implementation, versioned contract, verification evidence, operational signals, and rollback behavior described below are present. A mock screen, planning-only schema, permission constant, or passing unit test alone is not sufficient evidence.
</Callout>

## Why this milestone exists

The operator system is deployable, recoverable, observable, and promotable.

## Scope and deliverables

Commit executable manifests/overlays, image digests, migrations, probes, drain, resources, autoscaling, queue limits, rollout/rollback, canary, backup/PITR, restore, RPO/RTO, SLOs, chaos scenarios, SBOM, provenance, signatures, and admission verification.

## Contract and data requirements

Deployment metadata links image digest, schema/migration version, environment, rollout stage, and evidence artifact. Backup inventory covers Postgres, ClickHouse, Redis, object manifests, and evidence bundles.

## Product and UX requirements

Operators see dependency health, backup freshness, restore-drill age, ingestion lag, DLQ depth, saturation, and degraded state.

## Non-functional requirements

A clean restore meets declared RPO/RTO and preserves tenant isolation. Load, provider failure, queue, database, slow-client, chaos, and rollback gates pass.

## Verification gates

Run staging rollout, restore drill, signed artifact verification, and failure injection. Require evidence before promotion.

## Rollout and rollback

Promote dark → staging → canary. Roll back by known-good digest and compatibility-safe version.

## Findings addressed

This milestone addresses the repository and roadmap findings recorded in Track P and the Phase 4 findings register.

## Exit checklist

- [ ] The stated outcome is demonstrated against real or explicitly labeled deterministic fixtures.
- [ ] The API, event, schema, or configuration contract is versioned and covered by compatibility tests.
- [ ] Tenant, permission, privacy, freshness, and partial-data behavior are verified.
- [ ] Performance, accessibility, failure, and rollback evidence is attached to the milestone record.
- [ ] Documentation, runbooks, and links are updated before the milestone is marked complete.
