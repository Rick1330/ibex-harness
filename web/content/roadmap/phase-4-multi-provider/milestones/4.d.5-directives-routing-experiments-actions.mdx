# Milestone 4.D.5 — Directives, Routing, Experiments & Controlled Actions

**Status:** Planned  
**Outcome:** Operators understand and safely change policy with preview, approval, provenance, and rollback.  
**Track:** [Track D — Operator Capability Slices](/roadmap/phase-4-multi-provider/tracks#track-d--operator-capability-slices)  
**Dependencies:** 4.D.2; 4.D.4 recommended; 4.P.1 step-up; 4.P.2 routing decision + directive snapshot; 4.P.3 audit; Phase 4.5 fingerprinting for drift UI  
**Estimated effort:** 4 weeks

<Callout type="note" title="Completion contract">
  Drift UI stays behind a Phase 4.5 producer contract (F4-013). Directive/routing controls ship first.
</Callout>

## Why this milestone exists

Provider/policy mutations lack action journal, approval, preview, rollback, and decision provenance (F4-010, F4-026).

## Scope and deliverables

Immutable directive versions, diff, promotion/revoke state machine, regression-run linkage, matched policy revision, ordered candidates and rejection reasons, capability-catalog version, provider credential scope, fallback trigger, experiment assignment/exposure/holdback, guardrails, kill switch, operator action ledger, dry-run preview, bounded rollout, rollback pointer.

## Contract and data requirements

Replaying recorded inputs with recorded versions yields the same decision. Sensitive changes require step-up; high-impact changes may require dual approval. Duplicate submits safe; blast radius shown before confirmation.

## Product and UX requirements

Preview/approval UX; clear abort reasons; no drift alerts without producer evidence.

## Non-functional requirements

Dark launch → 1% shadow → 10% → 25% → 100% only after sample and bake windows. Automated abort on quality/latency/error/safety/cost guardrail breach.

## Verification gates

Deterministic replay of recorded decisions; action ledger completeness; abort/rollback drill.

## Rollout and rollback

**Trigger:** directive/routing abort, unsafe experiment promotion, or action-ledger inconsistency. **Reversal:** reactivate prior policy/directive version; disable new controlled actions. **Verify:** traffic uses prior version; aborted actions recorded; preview/approval fail closed until restored.

## Findings addressed

F4-010, F4-013, F4-026.

## Exit checklist

- [ ] Directive lifecycle + action ledger evidenced.
- [ ] Step-up/dual-approval paths tested.
- [ ] Staged rollout abort drill attached.
- [ ] Drift UI gated behind Phase 4.5 contract note.
