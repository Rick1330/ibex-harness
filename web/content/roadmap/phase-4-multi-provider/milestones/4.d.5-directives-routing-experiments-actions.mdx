# Milestone 4.D.5 — Directives, Routing, Experiments & Controlled Actions

**Status:** Planned  
**Outcome:** Operators understand and safely change policy with preview, approval, provenance, experimentation, and rollback.  
**Track:** [Track D — Operator Capability Slices](/roadmap/phase-4-multi-provider/tracks#track-d--operator-capability-slices)  
**Dependencies:** Track P gates and preceding Track D slice  
**Estimated effort:** 4 weeks

<Callout type="note" title="Completion contract">
  This milestone is complete only when the implementation, versioned contract, verification evidence, operational signals, and rollback behavior described below are present. A mock screen, planning-only schema, permission constant, or passing unit test alone is not sufficient evidence.
</Callout>

## Why this milestone exists

Operators understand and safely change policy with preview, approval, provenance, experimentation, and rollback.

## Scope and deliverables

Build directive versions/diff/promotion/revoke, routing decision detail, candidate rejection reasons, fallback provenance, evaluation linkage, experiment assignment/exposure/holdback, guardrails, kill switch, dry-run, action ledger, and bounded rollout.

## Contract and data requirements

Record policy/directive/capability versions, input hashes, matched candidates, rejection codes, approval, assurance, before/after, and rollback pointer.

## Product and UX requirements

Show blast radius, guardrails, policy state, simulation versus observed state, and explicit approval requirements.

## Non-functional requirements

Versioned replay reproduces decisions. High-impact changes require step-up or dual approval. Duplicate submits and rollback tests pass.

## Verification gates

Dark → 1% → 10% → 25% → 100% with abort thresholds. Drift UI depends on the Phase 4.5 producer contract.

## Rollout and rollback

The old milestone combined two unready products and lacked an action ledger.

## Findings addressed

This milestone addresses the repository and roadmap findings recorded in Track P and the Phase 4 findings register.
