# Milestone 4.P.1 — Identity, Tenancy & Authorization Assurance

**Status:** Planned  
**Outcome:** Every operator request, stream, export, deletion, replay, and asynchronous job has a verified tenant and policy decision.  
**Track:** [Track P — Operator Readiness, Evidence & Control Plane](/roadmap/phase-4-multi-provider/tracks#track-p--operator-readiness-evidence--control-plane)  
**Dependencies:** Track A identity/API foundations; Phase 3.5; 4.C.5 baseline  
**Estimated effort:** 3 weeks

<Callout type="note" title="Completion contract">
  This milestone is complete only when the implementation, versioned contract, verification evidence, operational signals, and rollback behavior described below are present. A mock screen, planning-only schema, permission constant, or passing unit test alone is not sufficient evidence.
</Callout>

## Why this milestone exists

Every operator request, stream, export, deletion, replay, and asynchronous job has a verified tenant and policy decision.

## Scope and deliverables

Implement session/refresh or OIDC federation seam, revocation, org membership, org switching, role/permission evaluation, resource classification, step-up authentication, and dual approval hooks for high-impact actions.

## Contract and data requirements

Define metadata-read, redacted-read, raw-read, export, delete, replay, secret-use, policy-change, and break-glass actions with actor, tenant, purpose, assurance, and policy result.

## Product and UX requirements

Denied actions explain the safe next step without revealing object existence. The UI never shows provider keys, bearer tokens, or decrypted secrets.

## Non-functional requirements

Normal dashboard roles cannot bypass RLS. Authorization is deny-by-default and evaluated server-side on every request.

## Verification gates

Run a two-tenant matrix across API, Postgres, Redis, ClickHouse, object storage, queues, exports, replay, guessed IDs, org switching, revoked sessions, expired step-up, and degraded identity provider.

## Rollout and rollback

Start read-only with internal operators. Roll back policy versions, never audit history. Disable sensitive actions with a kill switch.

## Findings addressed

This milestone addresses the repository and roadmap findings recorded in Track P and the Phase 4 findings register.
