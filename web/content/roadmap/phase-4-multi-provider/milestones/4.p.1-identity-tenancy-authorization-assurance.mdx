# Milestone 4.P.1 — Identity, Tenancy & Authorization Assurance

**Status:** mounted-but-provisional — hard dependency for operator E2E; not closed by scaffolding.
**Outcome:** Every request, stream, export, deletion, replay, secret use, and asynchronous job receives a server-derived tenant and policy decision.
**Track:** [Track P — Operator Readiness, Evidence & Control Plane](/roadmap/phase-4-multi-provider/tracks#track-p--operator-readiness-evidence--control-plane)
**Dependencies:** P0; Track A identity/API foundations. P1 must be evidenced before D0 or P6-bootstrap proceeds.
**Estimated effort:** 3 weeks
**Gate:** G1 identity

<Callout type="warning" title="Hard dependency">
  Operator readiness cannot proceed until RS256 session verification and rotation, server-side session lifecycle and revocation, server-derived organization context, mounted permission enforcement, and mounted step-up enforcement are exercised on real routers and jobs. Constants, migrations, middleware scaffolding, client-side checks, or an unmounted `require_operator_permission`/step-up probe do not close G1.
</Callout>

<Callout type="note" title="Current boundary">
  The baseline records policy epochs, deny-by-default model policy, secret-use and SSRF foundations, and dual-verify scaffolding, but does not by itself prove a mounted HTTP contract or a two-tenant operator matrix. Do not treat G1 as closed until P6 evidence exists.
</Callout>

## Required server-side contract

- Verify AuthService sessions with RS256 key rotation and revocation; provisional HMAC verification must not be reachable in shared environments.
- Derive `org_id` from the verified server session and membership, never from a client-controlled route, query, header, or browser storage value.
- Mount authentication, permission bits, resource/action classification, and deny-by-default policy checks on every API, SSE, export, mutation, and asynchronous worker path.
- Require fresh step-up for deletion, raw/export, production replay, secret changes, policy changes, break-glass, and other high-impact actions; expired, missing, or cross-tenant step-up must deny.
- Preserve explicit `org_id` filters in PostgreSQL, Redis, ClickHouse, object storage, queues, and read models.
- Keep policy epoch reconciliation fail-closed; narrow credential retrieval and block private-range or non-allowlisted provider URLs.

## Verification gate

P6-slice-certification must run a four-role/two-tenant matrix against the mounted runtime: viewer, operator, admin, and owner; Org B direct lookups return `404` where anti-enumeration applies and searches return empty; revoked sessions, expired step-up, policy-store outage, guessed IDs, org switching, and unscoped credential access deny. Evidence must identify the route or job, role, tenant, policy result, and artifact.

## Rollout and rollback

**Trigger:** tenant leak, stale allow, fail-open policy, revoked-session acceptance, or secret exposure. **Reversal:** disable sensitive actions, return to metadata-only/read-only mode, and roll back policy versions without rewriting audit history. **Verify:** rerun the two-tenant matrix and confirm fail-closed responses.

## Exit checklist

- [ ] RS256 session verification/rotation, lifecycle, and revocation are mounted and evidenced.
- [ ] Server-derived org context is enforced on API, SSE, export, mutation, and job paths.
- [ ] Permission checks are mounted server-side; client visibility is not an authorization control.
- [ ] Step-up is mounted for every listed high-impact action and expiry denies.
- [ ] Four-role/two-tenant negative matrix is attached through P6-slice-certification.
- [ ] Policy-store outage, SSRF, credential-scope, and stale-revocation negative tests pass.
