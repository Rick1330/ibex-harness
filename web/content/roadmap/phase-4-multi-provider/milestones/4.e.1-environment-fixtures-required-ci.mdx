# Milestone 4.E.1 — Environment, Fixtures & Required CI

**Status:** Planned  
**Outcome:** The intended operator environment runs authenticated, multi-tenant, accessible tests and publishes evidence.  
**Track:** [Track E — Release Readiness & Phase Exit](/roadmap/phase-4-multi-provider/tracks#track-e--release-readiness--phase-exit)  
**Dependencies:** Track P and Track D capability evidence  
**Estimated effort:** 2 weeks

<Callout type="note" title="Completion contract">
  This milestone is complete only when the implementation, versioned contract, verification evidence, operational signals, and rollback behavior described below are present. A mock screen, planning-only schema, permission constant, or passing unit test alone is not sufficient evidence.
</Callout>

## Why this milestone exists

The intended operator environment runs authenticated, multi-tenant, accessible tests and publishes evidence.

## Scope and deliverables

Provision browsers, API/data dependencies or contract doubles, four roles, two tenants, golden fixtures, Playwright, axe, keyboard, OpenAPI snapshot, reports, and required CI jobs.

## Contract and data requirements

Test manifests pin environment, fixture, commit, image, schema, and report paths.

## Product and UX requirements

Test the real navigation, data states, query persistence, trace inspector, memory, incident, policy, and cost journeys.

## Non-functional requirements

CI required checks pass; failures upload traces/reports and block merge/promotion.

## Verification gates

No release if the operator environment is unavailable; revert to last known-good evidence-backed release.

## Rollout and rollback

Replaces the old dashboard-only smoke assumption.

## Findings addressed

This milestone addresses the repository and roadmap findings recorded in Track P and the Phase 4 findings register.

## Exit checklist

- [ ] The stated outcome is demonstrated against real or explicitly labeled deterministic fixtures.
- [ ] The API, event, schema, or configuration contract is versioned and covered by compatibility tests.
- [ ] Tenant, permission, privacy, freshness, and partial-data behavior are verified.
- [ ] Performance, accessibility, failure, and rollback evidence is attached to the milestone record.
- [ ] Documentation, runbooks, and links are updated before the milestone is marked complete.
