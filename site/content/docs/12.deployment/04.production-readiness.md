## Must be true before promotion

### Identity and tenancy

- Hosted AuthService session, MFA, secure cookies, CSRF, and exact credentialed CORS are accepted.
- Two-tenant negative tests cover proxy, memory, context, management API, MCP, Operator Console, ClickHouse, Redis, and backups.
- Cross-tenant resource behavior is consistent and does not leak existence.
- Provider credentials are sealed and never returned in plaintext.

### Data and deletion

- Typed memory lifecycle and revision ledger are accepted.
- Deletion covers caches, projections, backups, and archives with measured timing.
- Restore drills prove the documented RPO/RTO.
- ClickHouse projection delivery and retention are verified, not inferred.

### Request path

- A hosted or reference-profile p99 run proves the latency budget with workload, hardware, and profile attached.
- The Go benchmark gate is not mistaken for a p99 SLO.
- Streaming failures, cancellation, and upstream timeouts are covered.
- Budget holds are reconciled and overshoot is measured.

### Evidence and operations

- The relay delivers to a production sink with dedupe/replay and redaction evidence.
- Dead letters, reaper behavior, and crash recovery are exercised.
- Key rotation and revocation propagation are tested.
- Alerts have runbooks and owners.
- Rollback by image digest is rehearsed.

### Deployment

- Real image digests replace sentinels.
- External Secrets are provisioned.
- Data-plane templates or managed services exist.
- Migrations run with a preflight and rollback plan.
- A deployment workflow and release approval path exist.
- NetworkPolicy and service-to-service authentication are configured.

## Explicit non-claims

- No hosted HA guarantee.
- No measured hosted p99.
- No exactly-once side effects.
- No complete tenant-isolation proof.
- No certified restore or RPO/RTO.
- No production Support/SLO contract.

When a gate closes, update the ledger first, then the page. A page cannot promote a capability by itself.
