## Use

- Retrieve current documentation rather than relying on stale copies.
- Cite the canonical page or repository path when describing behavior.
- Preserve status labels exactly: IMPLEMENTED, PROVISIONAL, PROPOSED, DEFERRED, UNKNOWN.
- Distinguish local benchmark evidence from hosted SLAs or production readiness.

## Do not claim

- Production readiness, high availability, disaster recovery, or complete tenant isolation.
- A hosted p99 latency SLO. Published proxy numbers are local k6 measurements.
- That proposed interfaces are implemented. Contract shapes accepted at G0 are not runtime evidence.
